## Detailed Program Analysis

### 1. Cybersecurity and Information Assurance - M.S.

**Target Roles:** Information Security Analyst, Security Architect, Penetration Tester, CISO

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Median Salary | $124,910 |
| Top 10% Earnings | $186,420+ |
| Management Track | $171,200 (Security Manager/CISO) |
| Job Growth | 29% (much faster than average) |
| Annual Openings | ~16,000 |
| Global Talent Gap | 4,000,000+ unfilled positions |
| Tuition/Term | $4,700 |
| Certs Included | CySA+, PenTest+, CASP+, CISM, CC |

**Strengths:** Highest job growth rate among established roles, massive global talent shortage, 5 industry certifications included (highest cert value of any program), strong alignment with NICE framework.

**Considerations:** Highest tuition per term ($4,700), but included certifications would cost $2,000-3,000+ if purchased separately.

## Overall Recommendation

### If you want the SINGLE BEST all-around pick:
**Cybersecurity and Information Assurance M.S.** - It has the strongest combination of job security (29% growth, 4M global shortage), included certifications ($2-3K+ value), fast completion (18 months), and strong salary trajectory. The field gets more important every year as digital threats increase.

### If you want the HIGHEST immediate salary:
**Information Technology M.S.** or **MBA IT Management** - Both target the $171,200 median IT manager role, but the M.S. IT is cheaper and faster.

### If you want the HIGHEST long-term ceiling:
**Computer Science M.S. (AI/ML specialization)** or **MBA IT Management** - CS with AI/ML can reach $300K-500K+ at top tech companies. MBA opens C-suite paths.

### If you want the FASTEST growing field:
**Data Analytics M.S.** - 34% growth rate is the highest of any program listed, though starting median is lower.

### If you want the MOST job openings:
**Computer Science M.S.** or **Software Engineering M.S.** - 129,000+ annual openings for software roles means the broadest job market.
